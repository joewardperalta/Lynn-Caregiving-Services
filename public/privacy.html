Privacy

Privacy Policy

Please note — this page should be reviewed by the business owner and, where appropriate, a qualified legal professional. It is a starting point and does not claim legal compliance that has not been verified.

This website is operated by Lynn’s Caregiving Services (“we”, “us”). This page explains, in general terms, how information submitted through the website may be used.

Information collected

When you submit a care request, contact form, or job application, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Preferred contact method
  • Relationship to the person who may need care
  • Type of support requested, schedule preferences, and additional comments you choose to share
  • For job applications: position preference, availability, preferred shifts, and relevant experience

We do not ask for diagnoses, health card numbers, Social Insurance Numbers, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information through these forms.

Why information is collected

We collect this information so we can understand the type of care you are looking for, respond to your inquiry, or consider a job application.

How inquiries are used

Submitted requests and applications are emailed to the business owner for follow-up. We use the information to contact you about the request or application you submitted.

Email communication

We may email you about your inquiry, including a confirmation that your request was received if that feature is enabled. We do not use your inquiry details to send unrelated marketing messages unless you have separately agreed to that.

Data retention

Inquiry and application details are kept only as long as needed to respond to the request and operate the business, unless a longer period is required for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website, including sending it over the server rather than exposing email credentials in the public website files. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
