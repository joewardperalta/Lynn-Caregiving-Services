Services

Support That Helps With Everyday Life at Home

Personal Support Workers provide respectful, non-medical assistance so clients can remain comfortable, safe, and independent at home.

Personal Care & Hygiene

Respectful assistance with personal routines while protecting privacy, dignity, comfort, and independence.

  • Bathing and showering
  • Skincare and grooming
  • Dressing
  • Oral hygiene
  • Toileting
  • Incontinence care

Mobility & Physical Support

Practical support to help clients move more safely and confidently within their home and daily routine.

  • Walking assistance
  • Transfers between bed, chair, or wheelchair
  • Wheelchair support
  • Gentle physical exercises or movement as directed by the care plan

Health Observation & Routine Support

Non-medical support that helps families stay informed and helps clients follow established routines.

  • Medication reminders
  • Observation of physical or behavioural changes
  • Updates to family members or the appropriate care contact when changes are noticed
  • Support with routines identified in the client’s care plan

Social & Emotional Care

Companionship and meaningful engagement can be just as important as practical assistance.

  • Conversation and companionship
  • Participation in personal hobbies and interests
  • Social engagement
  • Encouragement of dignity, choice, and independence

Meal Preparation & Feeding

Help with meals and daily nutrition based on the client’s preferences and dietary requirements provided in the care plan.

  • Meal planning
  • Light cooking and reheating
  • Support with specific dietary requirements
  • Feeding assistance when needed

Personal Support Workers provide non-medical support. This service is not a substitute for nursing or medical care. Available services may vary depending on individual needs, location, scheduling, and the client’s care plan.
